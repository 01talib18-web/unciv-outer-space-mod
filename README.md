# Outer Space / Stellar Empires Mod for Unciv

A detailed extension mod that brings outer space, sci-fi, and galactic empire-building to Unciv. Explore the stars, colonize new worlds, encounter alien civilizations, construct mega-structures, and pursue technological dominance or peaceful expansion.

## Features
- Multiple new civilizations with unique abilities focused on space exploration, colonization, research, and combat.
- Custom space-themed units: colonist ships, fighters, capital vessels, terraformers, and science vessels.
- Orbital and planetary buildings: habitats, shipyards, research outposts, Dyson segments, and terraforming arrays.
- Expanded technology options emphasizing FTL travel, AI, mega-engineering, and first contact.
- New policies for expansionism, technocracy, and stellar militarism.
- Rich flavor text and city/planet names evoking a vast universe.

## Installation
1. In Unciv, go to **Mods** > **Download mod from URL**.
2. Paste: `https://github.com/01talib18-web/unciv-outer-space-mod`
3. Download and enable the mod.
4. Start a new game — new civilizations and content will be available.

## Theme
This mod captures the wonder and conflict of space: discovering ancient ruins, allying with or conquering alien species, building Dyson swarms, and deciding the fate of galactic civilization. All mechanics are balanced for Unciv while staying true to sci-fi tropes.

## Compatibility
Extension mod (adds to vanilla). Works alongside other mods. Tested structure for stability.
